01

WHY I MADE THEM

My previous headset and mouse both broke after being thrown loose into a backpack for travel. When I replaced them with nicer hardware, I decided the new versions needed proper cases.

02

HEADPHONE CASE

The Arctis Nova case includes a felt-lined cavity, a dedicated slot for the wireless dongle, a separate compartment for the charging cable, three snap closures, foam feet, and a small top handle for carrying or hanging.

03

MOUSE CASE

The Razer Naga Pro case had to fit the mouse's unusual shape while also storing its interchangeable side-button panels. Magnets hold the panels in place, while a 3D printed bending latch closes the lid with a satisfying snap.
