01

THE IDEA

It started with a simple annoyance: a drill is one of the most important tools in a toolbox, but using one also means keeping track of a battery and a charger that inevitably end up somewhere else.

I wanted to fix that by integrating a drill-battery charger directly into the toolbox.

02

THE RABBIT HOLE

Once the toolbox needed a power connection, I started asking what else could be integrated.

  • Integrated drill battery charger
  • Torsion-spring retractable power cable
  • Integrated front power strip
  • Dimmable LED lighting

Each feature solved a problem — and created a new one to solve.

03

THE DETAILS

Dedicated storage keeps the tools I use most often immediately accessible.

  • Removable drill-bit case
  • Soldering iron + supplies
  • Drawing and cutting tools
  • Multimeter
  • Adhesives, tapes & fasteners

The lid also mounts a measuring tape, knife, caliper, and ruler for quick access.
